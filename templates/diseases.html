Respiratory Conditions

Diseases We Detect

Our AI classifies 4 categories of respiratory sound patterns linked to these common lung diseases. Learn what each condition means and how it sounds.

🌬️
Wheeze Pattern

Asthma

Asthma is a chronic condition where airways become inflamed and narrow, causing recurring episodes of wheezing, breathlessness, chest tightness, and coughing — especially at night or early morning.

300M worldwide
All ages
High-pitched wheeze

Key Symptoms

  • Wheezing
  • Chest tightness
  • Shortness of breath
  • Night cough
  • Breathlessness
🫁
Crackle Pattern

Pneumonia

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs, filling them with fluid or pus. It produces characteristic crackling sounds during inhalation — like walking on dry leaves.

450M per year
All, esp. elderly
Crackling pops

Key Symptoms

  • Fever & chills
  • Crackling sounds
  • Productive cough
  • Chest pain
  • Fatigue
💨
Wheeze Pattern

COPD

Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease is a group of progressive lung diseases — primarily emphysema and chronic bronchitis — that block airflow and make breathing increasingly difficult.

200M globally
40+ years
Persistent wheeze

Key Symptoms

  • Chronic cough
  • Wheezing
  • Breathlessness
  • Mucus overproduction
  • Frequent infections
🔴
Crackle Pattern

Bronchitis

Bronchitis is inflammation of the bronchial tubes that carry air to and from the lungs. The inflamed tubes produce excess mucus, causing a persistent cough with crackling and rattling sounds.

Acute / Chronic
All ages
Wet crackles

Key Symptoms

  • Persistent cough
  • Mucus / phlegm
  • Crackling sounds
  • Low-grade fever
  • Chest discomfort
